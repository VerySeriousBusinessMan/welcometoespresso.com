Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

The short version

Welcome to Espresso is a static informational website. We do not run accounts, collect sign-ups, or store your personal information on our servers. We don't sell data. The main privacy consideration is Amazon's affiliate tracking and Google Fonts, both explained below.

Information we collect directly

None. This site has no login, no newsletter, no contact forms, and no comment system. We do not collect your name, email address, or any other personal information directly.

Third-party services

This site uses third-party services for hosting, fonts, and affiliate tracking. These services may collect standard technical data such as IP addresses and browser information as part of their normal operation. We do not control these third parties and encourage you to review their privacy policies directly.

Cookies

This site itself does not set cookies. Third-party services we link to (such as affiliate retailers) may set cookies in your browser. We store your language preference (English or Spanish) in your browser's localStorage — this stays on your device and is never transmitted to us.

External links

Our guides link to products and resources on other websites. Once you leave welcometoespresso.com, this privacy policy no longer applies. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any sites you visit.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Email us at WelcometoEspresso@gmail.com.
