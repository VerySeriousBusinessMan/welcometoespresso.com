About this site

Welcome to Espresso

Why this site exists

Most espresso content online assumes you already know what you're doing. The forums are full of jargon, the YouTube videos start with "grind retention" before explaining what a portafilter is, and half the buying guides are quietly sponsored by the gear they're ranking.

Welcome to Espresso started because beginners deserve better than that. You shouldn't need three hours of research just to figure out what to buy first.

What we do

We write buying guides and how-to articles aimed at people who are new to home espresso. Everything is written in plain English, with prices, trade-offs, and honest opinions — not marketing copy.

We don't accept free products, sponsored placements, or paid reviews. The gear we recommend is gear we'd actually use.

How we make money

Welcome to Espresso participates in the Amazon Associates program. When you click a product link and buy something on Amazon, we earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. That's it — no ads, no sponsored content.

This commission is what keeps the site running and lets us keep the content free. It doesn't influence what we recommend. You can read the full details on our Affiliate Disclosure page.
