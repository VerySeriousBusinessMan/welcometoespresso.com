Best Espresso Machine for Lattes

8 min read Updated June 2026
The short version

For lattes, the steam wand matters more than anything else — that's what turns milk into the silky microfoam a latte needs. The Breville Bambino Plus (~$499) is the best beginner pick because its automatic steam wand textures milk for you. If you'd rather learn manual steaming or spend less, the De'Longhi Dedica Arte (~$199) is the value champion.

Any espresso machine can pull a shot. What separates a good latte machine from a mediocre one is whether it can steam milk into proper microfoam — the glossy, paint-like texture that pours into a latte instead of sitting on top as dry foam. So this guide ranks machines by how well (and how easily) they steam milk, not just by espresso quality.

01

What makes a machine good for lattes

Steam wand type. An automatic wand (like the Bambino Plus's) heats and textures milk to a set temperature on its own — ideal for beginners. A manual wand gives you full control but takes practice. A panarello wand (common on cheap machines) froths but produces airy, bubbly foam that's hard to make latte art with.

Steam power and recovery. More steam pressure means faster, easier texturing. Underpowered wands make it hard to build microfoam before the milk overheats.

Heat-up and switch time. Machines that go from shot to steam quickly (or do both at once) make the whole process smoother.

Notice that none of these are about espresso quality. A great latte machine and a great espresso machine overlap, but for milk drinks the wand is what you're really buying.

The best machines for lattes

Best for beginners Top pick

Breville Bambino Plus

~$499 on Amazon

The Bambino Plus is the easiest way to get café-quality milk at home. Its automatic steam wand textures milk to your chosen temperature hands-free — you fill the pitcher, submerge the wand, press a button, and it produces consistent microfoam even before you've learned manual technique. For latte drinkers who don't want a learning curve, nothing at this level beats it.

It also heats in 3 seconds and pulls excellent espresso from a 54mm portafilter, so the coffee under the milk is genuinely good. If lattes are your main drink, this is the one.

De'Longhi Dedica Arte

The Dedica Arte ships with De'Longhi's My LatteArt manual steam wand — a real wand (not a panarello) designed to make microfoam more achievable for beginners. What to avoid for lattes

Panarello-only machines. Many cheap machines have a panarello (a wand with a plastic frothing sleeve). It makes airy foam for a quick cappuccino but can't make the smooth microfoam a latte needs. If milk drinks matter, avoid panarello-only wands.

Steam-less pod machines. Most Nespresso-style machines either have no steam wand or a separate frother that makes dry foam. If lattes are the goal, a real steam wand is non-negotiable — see our Nespresso vs. espresso comparison.
