Best Espresso Scales for Beginners

5 min read Why weighing your shot changes everything

A scale turns espresso from guesswork into a repeatable process. You weigh your dose (ground coffee in), pull the shot, and weigh your yield (liquid espresso out). Once you hit a ratio that tastes good, you can hit it again — every time. Without a scale, you're chasing flavor by feel alone.

Espresso scales need to respond fast — you're often timing a 25–30 second extraction and adjusting on the fly. All three picks below have sub-second response times and a built-in timer, which is all you actually need.

What to look for

For espresso: 0.1g precision, fast response time (under 500ms), and a built-in timer. Waterproofing is a bonus but rarely critical for home use. You don't need Bluetooth or flow rate unless you're going deep on pour-over too.

Best overall: Timemore Black Mirror Basic Plus

Timemore Black Mirror Basic Plus

~$55 on Amazon

The Timemore Black Mirror is the scale that home baristas keep recommending to each other, and the Basic Plus earns that reputation. It reads in 0.1g increments with a fast, stable response — fast enough to track espresso extraction in real time. The auto-timer starts when it detects the weight of your portafilter and the first drops of espresso landing, so you don't have to juggle timer and scale buttons during the pull.

Rechargeable via USB-C with a 1600mAh battery rated for ~10 hours of use. The flat matte surface is easy to keep clean. No frills, nothing to learn — it just works.

Most reliable: Hario V60 Drip Scale

Hario V60 Drip Scale

~$55 on Amazon

The Hario scale has been the default recommendation for coffee beginners for over a decade, and it's still a completely solid choice. It's simple to operate, has two buttons, a clear display, and a timer that starts with a single tap. The updated model has improved response time and water resistance over the original.

The Timemore edges it out on response speed and auto-timer, but if you want something dead-simple with a long track record, the Hario is reliable and well-supported.

Best premium: Felicita ARC

Felicita ARC Coffee Scale

~$75 on Amazon

The Felicita ARC is what you upgrade to when you want more from your scale. It's IPX5 waterproof — so spills and steam aren't a concern — has an ultra-fast response time, and includes flow rate measurement alongside the timer. For beginners, the extra features can feel like overkill. But if you want a scale that grows with your setup and never needs replacing, the ARC is worth the $20 premium over the Timemore.
